archi-crab shared observability backends.

This chart deliberately ships as a thin wrapper. Grafana, Prometheus and
Langfuse are upstream charts with large value surfaces; they are declared as
Argo CD Applications in argocd/archi-crab/shared/ rather than vendored here,
so their versions are pinned where every other upstream chart is pinned.

This chart owns only what is genuinely ours:
  - the shared archi-crab-obs namespace
  - Grafana datasource wiring (below)
  - the dashboards, once Phase 8 tells us which questions to answer

Verify after sync:
  kubectl -n {{ .Values.namespace }} get pods
  kubectl -n {{ .Values.namespace }} get pvc      # all Bound, none Pending
